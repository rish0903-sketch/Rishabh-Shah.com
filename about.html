About Me

Chemical Engineer | Biotech & Fintech Enthusiast

My Journey

I'm a Chemical Engineer with a deep passion for innovation at the intersection of biotechnology and financial technology. My journey has been driven by a curiosity to understand how engineering principles can be applied to solve complex problems in emerging industries.

With a strong foundation in chemical engineering, I've developed a unique perspective that allows me to bridge traditional engineering practices with cutting-edge technologies. I'm fascinated by how biotech can revolutionize healthcare and how fintech can democratize financial services.

My Passion: Biotech

The biotechnology industry represents the future of healthcare and life sciences. I'm excited about how engineering principles can be applied to develop innovative solutions that improve lives, from pharmaceutical development to medical device design.

My Interest: Fintech

Financial technology is transforming how we interact with money and financial services. I'm drawn to how engineering approaches can create more accessible, efficient, and secure financial systems that benefit everyone.

My Approach

I believe in combining analytical thinking with creative problem-solving. Whether working on a biotech project or exploring fintech solutions, I apply systematic engineering methodologies while staying open to innovative approaches.

My goal is to contribute to projects that make a meaningful impact, whether that's advancing healthcare through biotechnology or improving financial accessibility through technology. I'm always eager to learn, collaborate, and push the boundaries of what's possible.
